# Keep personal planning inputs private

Keep each student's budget caps, other raw constraints, and personal reasons private to that student and their own agent; other agents and shared planning context receive acceptable proposals and counteroffers instead. This gives up the simplicity of a planner that sees every input to limit what other participants see directly, though offers and refusals can still reveal clues. This records intended product policy, not a claim of technical isolation, encryption, or privacy from the app or model provider.
