# I'm in if...

This glossary defines terms for friends making social plans with help from personal agents.

## Language

**Personal agent**:
A representative of one person in planning.

**Participant**:
A person considering joining a plan.

**Plan**:
A proposed social activity friends are considering together.

**Initial interest**:
A signal from at least one person that they are interested in doing something. Other people do not need to be interested yet.

**Tentative agreement**:
A plan the agents have negotiated that remains subject to approval by the people involved.

**Confirmation**:
The people's explicit approval of the final activity, time, estimated cost, and attendees.
