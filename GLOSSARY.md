# I'm in if...

This glossary defines terms for friends making social plans with help from personal agents.

## Language

**Personal agent**:
A representative of one person in planning.

**Participant**:
A person considering joining a plan.

**Plan**:
A proposed social activity friends are considering together.

**Initial interest**:
A signal from at least one person that they are interested in doing something. Other people do not need to be interested yet.

**Condition**:
A participant's preference or requirement that affects whether they will attend or which activity they will accept. Examples include availability, spending limits, activities to include or avoid, and who attends.

**Hard condition**:
A strict limit the participant requires a plan to satisfy. Only that participant may explicitly relax it.

**Flexible preference**:
A preference the participant allows their agent to compromise on, as long as the compromise stays within the participant's hard conditions.

**Attendance dependency**:
A condition linking one participant's attendance to a named person's attendance.

**Minimum group size**:
A condition that sets the number of people needed for a participant to join.

**Decision deadline**:
A time set by the initiator for deciding whether a willing group can proceed.

**Tentative agreement**:
A plan the agents have negotiated that remains subject to approval by the people involved.

**Confirmation**:
A participant's explicit approval of the activity, time, estimated cost, and attendee list. Their own agent asks for approval; silence does not count.
