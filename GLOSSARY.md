# I'm in if...

This glossary defines terms for friends making social plans with help from personal agents.

## Language

**Personal agent**:
A representative of one person in planning.

**Participant**:
A person considering joining a plan.

**Starter**:
The participant who begins a plan and may set its initial direction. Once invitees are admitted, they should have a say in later choices.

**Plan**:
A proposed social activity friends are considering together.

**Outing**:
One social plan a starter begins, together with the private chats attached to it. Conditions and agent memory do not carry over from another outing.

**Private chat**:
The saved conversation between one participant and their own personal agent, scoped to one outing and visible only to that participant.

**Initial interest**:
A signal from at least one person that they are interested in doing something. Other people do not need to be interested yet.

**Condition**:
A participant's preference or requirement that affects whether they will attend or which activity they will accept. Examples include availability, spending limits, activities to include or avoid, and who attends.

**Option ranking**:
A participant's private ordering of workable options, which their agent uses in negotiation. It is not approval of an option.

**Hard condition**:
A strict limit the participant requires a plan to satisfy. Only that participant may explicitly relax it.

**Flexible preference**:
A preference the participant allows their agent to compromise on, as long as the compromise stays within the participant's hard conditions.

**Attendance dependency**:
A condition linking one participant's attendance to a named person's attendance.

**Minimum group size**:
A condition that sets the number of people needed for a participant to join.

**Response cutoff**:
An optional starter-set deadline for collecting interest. When set, it determines when unanswered participants are excluded from the current proposed group; it does not delay confirmation. Without a cutoff, silence alone does not automatically exclude a participant or explain why they have not replied.

**Tentative agreement**:
A plan the agents have negotiated that remains subject to approval by the people involved.

**Confirmation**:
A participant's explicit approval of the activity, time, estimated cost, and attendee list. Their own agent asks for approval; silence does not count.

**Final approval deadline**:
The shared deadline for everyone included in a proposal to confirm it, at the proposed activity's start time. Everyone must explicitly approve the same current proposal before that time; changing its details requires fresh approval.

**Email invitation**:
A starter's invitation to a specific email address that grants admission when the recipient signs in with that verified address. It does not express the recipient's interest or confirm attendance.

**Admission request**:
A signed-in invitee's request for the starter to admit them to the planning group. Until approved, they cannot see group details or join negotiation; admission alone does not make them an attendee or confirm attendance.

**Join request**:
A request by an admitted planning-group participant to add themself to the attendee list of a confirmed plan, separate from an admission request. It leaves the existing plan unchanged until everyone in the proposed revised group meets their conditions and explicitly approves it.
